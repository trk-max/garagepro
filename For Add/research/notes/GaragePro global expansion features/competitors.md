# Competitor Feature Sets: Garage / Auto Repair Shop Management Software (as of late 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-01. Sources are mostly vendor pages, review aggregators (Capterra, G2, Software Advice), press releases and competitor comparison pages. Many price figures come from third-party aggregators and conflict with each other; conflicts are flagged. Note: tours.tekmetric.com (Tekmetric's FAQ/AI info page) could not be fetched from this session (network allow-list), so Tekmetric details are from search snippets.

## 1. Feature sets, pricing and segments of leading products

### Takeaway
North American leaders (Tekmetric, Shopmonkey, AutoLeap) are cloud SaaS priced at about USD 180-450 per month per location, with unlimited users. They compete on DVI, two-way texting, integrated payments/financing, marketing, and in 2026 AI phone/receptionist products. UK/AU/EU products are cheaper (about GBP 145 or AUD 99-249 per month) or modular/quote-based. They differentiate on local technical data (Autodata), accounting sync (Xero/Sage) and OEM/importer interfaces (Germany).

### Inferences
- The US price band is about USD 180-450 per location per month. The UK/AU band is about GBP 145 or AUD 99-249. Unlimited users is now the norm among US leaders (Tekmetric). Workshop Software and Garage Hive charge per user or per site.
- In both the US and the EU, the moat is integrated data: labour times (Mitchell/Motor/Autodata), plate/VIN decoding (AAA in France), OEM parts prices (Golda) and parts-supplier catalogs. GaragePro's closest analogue is a Tunisian plate/VIN decoder plus local parts-distributor catalogs.
- The Maghreb market looks underserved by dedicated, localized SaaS. Search found French desktop/SaaS packages (EBP Maroc) and generic ERPs, but no Tunisian-specific product.

## 2. Common feature categories, and table stakes vs differentiators (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
DVI with photo/video, two-way SMS with digital approval, online booking, canned jobs, a labour guide, parts ordering, integrated payments and accounting sync are table stakes at mid-tier and above. The 2026 differentiators are AI phone answering/receptionists, call scoring, integrated BNPL financing, built-in marketing and ads, telephony, and payment dispute tooling.

### Cited Findings

Base tier includes reminders and a customer portal. — [Xero App Store](https://apps.xero.com/au/industry/automotive/app/workshop-software)

### Inferences
- **Table stakes (2026):**
  - ROs, estimates and invoices
  - Customer/vehicle history (VIN, plate, mileage)
  - Canned jobs
  - Scheduling with online booking
  - Two-way SMS or WhatsApp with digital approval
  - DVI with photo/video
  - Inventory/stock
  - Tech time clock
  - Reminders
  - Accounting export
  - KPI dashboard (ARO, car count, gross profit, effective labor rate)
  - Mobile tech app
  - Unlimited users/ROs in US-style pricing
- **Differentiators:**
  - AI receptionist and call scoring
  - Embedded financing/BNPL
  - Integrated payments with dispute handling
  - Ads and marketing automation
  - Native telephony
  - Integrated labour-time/OEM data
  - Real-time parts-supplier catalogs and ordering
  - Multi-shop roll-up
  - Fleet accounts
- **Implications for Tunisia:**
  - WhatsApp likely replaces SMS as the messaging channel.
  - Local installment/credit or cheque handling may replace Sunbit/Affirm.
  - Arabic/French bilingual support and Tunisian invoicing/tax compliance may be the key local differentiators.
  - None of these Tunisia points is sourced here.

### Gaps
- I found no survey quantifying feature adoption rates. The "table stakes" classification is inferred from tier placement and launches.
- Not researched in depth: AI estimate generation, AI service writer and AI DVI summarization. Tekmetric "Smart DVI" details are unknown.
- Fleet-account and warranty-tracking features were not specifically sourced for each vendor.

## 3. User review themes (praise and complaints)

### Takeaway
Users praise ease of use, communication/texting and DVI workflows. They complain about price and add-on creep, support response times (Shopmonkey), reporting depth and inventory/core tracking (Tekmetric), flaky parts integrations, and buggy updates (Workshop Software).

### Cited Findings
- Ratings: Tekmetric is 4.7 on Capterra and 4.9 on G2 (about 490 reviews). Shopmonkey is 4.7 on Capterra (405 reviews) and 4.6 on G2. — [Capterra comparison](https://www.capterra.com/resources/tekmetric-vs-shopmonkey/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/shopmonkey-vs-tekmetric)
- Shopmonkey G2 pros: Ease of Use (17 mentions) and Communication (12). Cons: Limited Functionality (8) and Missing Features (8). Over the past 24 months, Capterra support sentiment is roughly even between positive and negative, with complaints about response times. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/shopmonkey-vs-tekmetric); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/resources/tekmetric-vs-shopmonkey/)
- Both Tekmetric and Shopmonkey draw "real, specific pricing complaints". — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/resources/tekmetric-vs-shopmonkey/)
- Tekmetric complaints:
  - Reporting, especially accounting-side reporting
  - Weak inventory core tracking that forces workarounds
  - A PartsTech integration that "seemed to have issues all the time"
  - Customer and internal communication tools that need upgrading
  - A labor guide that needs double-checking
  - Data migration from Shopmonkey that lost mileage
  Many of these reviews are from 2019-2021. — [Capterra reviews](https://capterra.com/p/190952/Tekmetric/reviews/); [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/Tekmetric/reviews)
- Workshop Software reviews criticize slow feature rollout and frequent updates causing problems. — [Xero App Store](https://apps.xero.com/au/industry/automotive/app/workshop-software)
- iDGARAGES consumer reviews say quotes rose at the garage, a trust risk for marketplace pricing. — [Avis Vérifiés](https://www.avis-verifies.com/avis-clients/idgarages.com?p=2njas)
- Garage Hive: per-user pricing is called a significant cost for very small shops (from a competitor source). Support hours are not published. — [Workshop Software vs Garage Hive](https://workshopsoftware.com/workshop-software-vs-garage-hive/)

### Inferences
- GaragePro opportunities:
  - Transparent all-inclusive pricing with no add-on creep
  - Fast local support in Arabic and French
  - Strong reporting and accounting exports
  - Robust core and used-parts tracking
  - Reliable data import from spreadsheets and paper
  - Quote accuracy and locking, which avoids the iDGARAGES-style trust problem

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/AutoRepair, r/MechanicAdvice) threads surfaced in the searches, so the Reddit sentiment requested is uncovered.
- No review data was found for Mitchell 1, Shop-Ware, Garage Hive, MAM, LOCO-Soft, WERBAS or the MENA vendors.
