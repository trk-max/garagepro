# Regional Automotive Aftermarket & Garage Market (Tunisia, Maghreb, MENA/Gulf, SSA, Francophone Europe) — Vendor View, 2024-2026

Research method note: Web search only. Direct page fetches failed for trade.gov and lapresse.tn because the session's network settings block those hosts. Findings below come from search-result snippets attributed to the linked pages; where a snippet was the only evidence, this is flagged. The research date is 2026-11-01.

## Tunisia: vehicle parc, garages, parts, insurance, inspection, IT, payments, regulation

### Takeaway
Tunisia has an old car parc (about half of vehicles are over 15 years old, July 2025) of roughly 2.3M officially registered vehicles (2021 figure; no 2025 official total found). It is growing again: about 93k vehicles were sold in 2025, plus about 30k through the parallel market. Two 2025-2026 regulatory shocks change what garage software has to do. The cheque reform (Feb 2025) pushed payments to cash. E-invoicing via TTN El Fatoora was extended to VAT-registered service providers from 1 Jan 2026, which likely includes many formal garages. The FTUSA E-Constat app (Nov 2025) also digitises the start of insurance claims.

### Inferences
- The best wedge is compliance plus cash. Formal VAT-registered garages need El Fatoora-compatible invoicing (TEIF XML/TTN connection, or export to a TTN-certified provider) from 2026. After the cheque reform they also need cash-drawer reconciliation, deposits/acomptes, instalment tracking ("crédit client") and lettre-de-change tracking.
- The old parc (more than 50% over 15 years) and high counterfeit prevalence argue for: parts-origin tagging (OEM / aftermarket / used / customer-supplied), per-part warranty tracking, photo evidence of parts swapped, and a VIN/plate-based history.
- E-Constat produces a PDF with a QR code. A garage tool that can attach the e-Constat PDF to a repair order and produce expert-ready quotes (labour hours, paint, parts with photos) would fit the insurer/expert loop. A "devis assurance" status workflow (waiting for expert, accord, franchise to collect) is a strong local feature.
- The ATTT inspection cycle gives a natural reminder trigger: "visite technique" due date reminders and pre-inspection checks, sent over WhatsApp/SMS.
- Online payment rails exist (Konnect, Flouci, D17 e-Dinar), but adoption at garages is unproven. Offer them as optional "pay link" features, not core.
- Pricing anchor: the Précisium franchise fee of 550 TND/month suggests organised garages pay meaningful fees for network services. Standalone SaaS for independents likely has to be far lower (tens of TND per month). That is an inference, with no direct benchmark found.

### Gaps
- No 2025 official parc total (ATTT/INS) or average age found; INS/ATTT pages were not reachable.
- No count of garages (formal or informal) or of mechanics, and no national mechanics' chamber data.
- No 2025 brand share data captured (automobile.tn 2025 registration statistics exist but were not fetched).
- No data on the share of used vs new parts, or on the main importers' market shares.
- Expert/assurance workflow beyond e-Constat (delays, mandatory expertise thresholds, IDA convention) was not found.
- No sources on garage IT usage, WhatsApp/Facebook usage by garages, internet reliability, Darija/French/Arabic usage in business software, or garage labour regulations. These need primary interviews or other sources.
- Facebook/marketplace offers for garage software could not be searched with these tools.
- No Konnect/Flouci merchant counts or fees.

## Morocco, Algeria, Libya, Egypt

### Takeaway
Evidence gathered was thin. Morocco's new-car market is growing strongly (+36% in H1 2025). Algeria is recovering from the 2017 import freeze. French DMS vendors (Solware Auto, incadea) and OEM-linked networks (Eurorepar) are present in Morocco and Algeria, and MeriGarage lists Egypt among its countries.

### Cited Findings
- Moroccan new-car sales were up 36.14% YoY in H1 2025 (112,026 units). — [Financial Afrik, Jul 2025](https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2025/07/03/morocco-new-car-market-grows-by-36-14-in-the-first-half-of-2025/)
- Algeria halted new vehicle imports in 2017 and closed assembly plants in 2019. — [J2R Auto](https://j2rauto.com/reseaux/euro-repar-car-service-sinstalle-en-algerie)
- Eurorepar Car Service is expanding in Algeria using Solware Auto's DMS. Eurorepar's older targets were 100 sites in Algeria and 50 in Morocco by 2021. — [J2R Auto](https://j2rauto.com/reseaux/eurorepar-car-service-se-developpe-en-algerie-avec-solware-auto); [GlobalFleet](https://www.globalfleet.com/fr/manufacturers/africa-middle-east/article/euro-repar-car-service-set-shop-morocco)
- Moroccan distributor Sopriam chose the incadea DMS with Solware Auto for implementation. — [Solware](https://www.solware.fr/?p=5554)
- MeriGarage (garage SaaS) lists Egypt among its available countries. — [Capterra AE](https://www.capterra.ae/software/1081985/MeriGarage)
- Autochek (Nigeria) considered expanding to Egypt and Algeria, among others (2020). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/11/18/nigerias-autochek-raises-3-4m-for-car-sales-and-service-platform/)

### Inferences
- Morocco and Algeria share Tunisia's French/Arabic business language mix and Stellantis/Renault-heavy brand base, so a Tunisian product should transfer well. The competition to beat at dealer level is French DMS (Solware, incadea), which leaves independents underserved.
- Egypt is more Arabic/English. It likely needs an Arabic-first UI with English, and different payment rails (e.g. Fawry, InstaPay; not researched here).

### Gaps
- No parc sizes, garage counts, local competitors, e-invoicing rules (Morocco DGI and Egypt ETA e-invoice/e-receipt) or payment habits were captured for Morocco, Algeria, Libya or Egypt. Libya had no data at all.

## Gulf (Saudi Arabia, UAE)

### Takeaway
The KSA repair market is sizeable (about USD 2.3B in 2025 per a commercial report). Software there is shaped by ZATCA phase-2 e-invoicing, and Arabic/ZATCA-ready accounting and workshop tools (Qoyod, Wafeq, Zoho, local systems sold on Haraj) already compete. No Saudization quota specific to repair workshops was found. Localisation rules exist for O&M jobs and vehicle inspection (MVPI) outlets.

### Cited Findings
- KSA repair and maintenance market is about USD 2.34B in 2025, projected to reach USD 3.49B by 2031 (commercial forecast). — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/ksa-automotive-repair-and-maintenance-market)
- The KSA aftersales service market was forecast at SAR 2.5B by 2025 (Ken Research press release, Feb 2024). — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/saudi-arabia-automotive-aftersales-service-market-poised-for-robust-growth-with-sar-2-5-billion-valuation-by-2025-ken-research-302050712.html)
- 93% of employers reported technical skills gaps in 2025 (commercial report; treat with caution). — [MarqStats](https://marqstats.com/reports/saudi-arabia-ev-maintenance-market/)
- Automechanika Riyadh 2025 focused on technician upskilling for hybrid servicing. — [Messe Frankfurt, Apr 2025](https://automechanika-riyadh.ae.messefrankfurt.com/riyadh/en/press/press-releases/2025/20250424.html)
- O&M localisation decision 35490 (Oct 2019) set targets above 36%. 34.79% of the targeted O&M jobs were Saudised by 2023. — [Gulf Migration](https://gulfmigration.grc.net/saudi-arabialocalization-ratio-for-the-operation-and-maintenance-contracts/); [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1697654)
- MVPI vehicle inspection outlets were localised in two 50% phases (HRSD guide on 7 activities, Sept 2023). — [HRSD](https://hrsd.gov.sa/en/node/1128989)
- Saudization rose for engineering roles (30%) from Jan 2026, which shows continued tightening. — [EY Tax News, Jan 2026](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0167-saudi-arabia-announces-increase-in-saudization-rates-for-roles-in-the-engineering-and-procurement-sectors)
- Qoyod, a Saudi cloud accounting product, markets workshop and car-maintenance-centre features: per-job-card costing, parts inventory linked to jobs, insurance-claim aging, and ZATCA e-invoicing (simplified B2C plus B2B to fleets/insurers). It advises choosing a ZATCA phase-2 approved vendor. — [Qoyod](https://www.qoyod.com/en/fields/accounting-software-for-car-maintenance-centers/)
- Other ZATCA-oriented options include Wafeq, Zoho Invoice SA and Enroutech (UAE/KSA workshop system), plus local workshop programs sold on Haraj. — [Wafeq](https://www.wafeq.com/en-sa); [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/sa/invoice/e-invoicing-solution); [Enroutech](https://enroutech.com/workshop-management-system/); [Haraj listing](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11176346528/Car_Workshop_Management_Program_Comprehensive_Accounting_Rep/)
- UAE garage ERP vendors (e.g. Emeron) market ERP adoption by UAE garages (vendor marketing). — [Emeron](https://emeron.io/?p=990355)

### Inferences
- Entering KSA requires ZATCA phase-2 integration (or a partnership with an approved provider), an Arabic RTL UI and printing, and insurer/fleet B2B invoicing. Arabic-first accounting incumbents (Qoyod, Wafeq) set the bar, so a specialised workshop UX plus integrations should be the differentiator.
- Workforce localisation may favour software that documents staff roles, training and technician productivity, but no direct workshop quota was confirmed.

### Gaps
- No UAE market size, no confirmed Saudization rule for workshops, and no Gulf pricing benchmarks were found.

## Sub-Saharan Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, South Africa)

### Takeaway
The aftermarket is highly informal and mobile-money driven: USD 1.4T flowed through mobile money in SSA in 2025. Startups so far focus on marketplaces and on-demand mechanics (Mecho, Fixit45, SparePap) rather than workshop SaaS, which leaves a gap for a simple, offline-capable, mobile-money-integrated tool. Francophone West Africa (Abidjan, Dakar) is a natural fit for a French product.

### Cited Findings
- SSA processed USD 1.4T of mobile money in 2025, out of more than USD 2T globally. SSA plus North Africa held 1.2B of 2.3B registered accounts, and MENA had 33 live services (GSMA SOTIR 2026). — [Connecting Africa](https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/-1-4t-flowed-through-mobile-money-in-sub-saharan-africa-in-2025-gsma)
- SSA smartphone penetration is about 33% (older GSMA-linked figure). GSMA had forecast 66% by 2025, but that is a projection, not an observed figure. — [ITWeb](https://itweb.africa/article/sub-saharan-africas-smartphone-penetration-at-33/RgeVDMPYAN5qKJN3)
- Nigeria's aftersales market is fragmented and informal, at about USD 8B. Mecho Autotech says it has onboarded more than 7,000 third-party mechanics. — [YC: Mecho Autotech](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mecho-autotech); [Malay Mail 2023](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/mediaoutreach/2023/05/03/mecho-autotech-to-launch-version-20-of-vehicle-management-app-and-new-automotive-spare-parts-marketplace-in-3q-/217815)
- Fixit45 raised USD 1.9M to expand into Kenya and Uganda. Autochek raised USD 3.4M (2020) with plans for Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, South Africa, Kenya, Egypt and Algeria. — [Daba Finance](https://dabafinance.com/fr/apprendre/mises-a-jour/fixit45-raises-1-9m-for-east-africa-expansion); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/11/18/nigerias-autochek-raises-3-4m-for-car-sales-and-service-platform/)
- Garage Mobility (Ghana) offers stock and shop-management software for parts distributors and repair shops across Africa. — [AU Startups](https://au-startups.com/fr/startups/garage-mobility)
- A Ugandan mechanic app built mobile-money wallets because garages want cash upfront. — [Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/business/auto/app-bringing-mechanic-services-close-1761866)
- Vendor blogs promote garage software with M-Pesa, Wave and Orange Money invoicing for Nairobi and Abidjan (marketing). — [Kolonell Nairobi](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/auto-repair-garage-invoice-app-nairobi-2026); [Kolonell Abidjan](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-atelier-mecanique-garage-abidjan-2026)

### Inferences
- An SSA SKU needs: offline-first PWA/Android, M-Pesa / Orange Money / Wave / MTN MoMo payment links, WhatsApp invoices, and very low or per-job pricing. Partnering with parts distributors (as Garage Mobility does) is a likely route to market.

### Gaps
- No country-level garage counts, no South Africa data (e.g. RMI membership) and no SSA SaaS price points were found.

## Pricing benchmarks, willingness to pay, distribution

### Takeaway
Few hard regional benchmarks exist. The data points found are the Précisium franchise (15,000 TND entry plus 550 TND/month), French SaaS (EBP MECA PRO subscription; one-time licences around €99), and US garage SaaS around USD 100/month. Distribution through franchise networks, insurers/assistance companies, parts distributors and accountants appears to be the main lever.

### Cited Findings
- Précisium Tunisia charges a 15,000 TND entry fee and 550 TND/month from year 2. — [J2R Auto](https://j2rauto.com/non-classe/precisium-simplante-en-tunisie)
- US garage SaaS benchmark is USD 100/month for 4-5 users. A French one-time licence product sells for €99. — [Spendbase](https://test.spendbase.co/fr/?p=40594) (search-snippet level evidence)
- EBP MECA PRO is sold as SaaS subscription in France. — [EBP brochure 2025](https://ebp.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ebp-logiciel-ebp-meca-pro-2025-bd.pdf)

## Diaspora / francophone Europe (France, Belgium)

### Takeaway
France is a large, mature market: about 69k workshops (Scalon), of which 83.7% are independent. Incumbents are entrenched (Fiducial, EBP, Solware). The opportunity for a French/Arabic product is a niche of independent and diaspora-owned garages, not a broad attack.

### Cited Findings
- Scalon counts 69,062 workshops in metropolitan France. 83.7% are independent (57,784) and manufacturer networks are 16.3%. Only 10,893 carry an identified network banner. — [Journal de l'Automobile](https://journalauto.com/distribution/les-reseaux-constructeurs-ne-representent-que-163-des-garages-francais/); [Auto-Infos](https://www.auto-infos.fr/article/garages-seulement-3-mra-sur-10-sous-enseigne-le-surprenant-decompte-de-scalon.293598)
- In 2025, vehicles visiting independent repairers averaged 12 years old, versus 7.6 years at OEM networks. — [Auto-Infos](https://www.auto-infos.fr/article/les-garages-independants-restent-le-premier-choix-des-automobilistes-francais-en-2025.292514)
- Independent repairers (MRA) and multi-brand networks hold 36.4% of the consumer parts market (FIEV barometer 2025). — [FIEV](https://www.fiev.fr/media/2025/10/Communiqué-de-presse_baromètre-aftermarket-EA-2025.pdf)
- Incumbent French vendors include Fiducial DMS Pro and EBP MECA PRO. — [Fiducial](https://www.fiducial.fr/Automobile-Motocycle-et-Machinisme-Agricole/Logiciel-automobile)

### Inferences
- France's own B2B e-invoicing mandate (from Sept 2026) would require PDP/PA certification or a partnership. That is from background knowledge and was not verified in this research, so it needs checking.

### Gaps
- No Belgium data and no data on diaspora-owned garages were found.
