# More ideas for GaragePro (not yet in the prompts)

Ideas beyond the 81 prompts, to discuss after the pilot. ★ = strong fit for Tunisia.

## For the garage owner
- ★ **Yard map**: where each car is parked (bay, outside, waiting for parts), key tag number, photo of the key board.
- **Smart key locker / drop box**: client drops keys after hours, gets a code; pick-up after payment.
- ★ **Waste register**: used oil, batteries, filters, tyres — quantities, collector, receipts (ANGed obligations), report.
- **Tool and equipment management**: diagnostic tools, lifts, torque wrenches — owner, maintenance and calibration dates.
- **Supplier returns and core charges** (exchange parts like starters, alternators, injectors).
- **Off-peak pricing**: discounts on quiet days/hours shown in online booking to fill the agenda.
- **No-show protection**: small deposit by payment link for bookings, refunded or deducted.
- **Bay rental**: rent a bay and tools by the hour to freelance mechanics or DIY clients.
- **Energy and water tracking** for car washes (meter readings, cost per wash, savings).
- **Mechanic training and certificates**: courses done, quizzes, link with the skills matrix and EV levels.

## For the mechanic
- ★ **Voice notes in Darija** turned into text on the work order (diagnosis, parts needed) — hands stay free.
- **AI photo help**: photo of a part or a leak → suggested cause and quote lines to confirm.
- **Torque specs and procedures** at hand from the repair-data partner (V8).
- **Gamification**: weekly goals (comebacks avoided, efficiency), shared on the TV screen.

## For the client
- ★ **Vehicle passport**: full history (repairs, washes, inspections, km) shareable by QR when selling the car —
  with the owner's consent, across all GaragePro garages.
- ★ **Pre-purchase inspection report**: a standard paid checklist for people buying a used car, printable certificate.
- ★ **Annual reminders beyond the garage**: vignette (car tax), insurance renewal, technical inspection, driving licence.
- **Pay later / instalments** through a Tunisian bank or microfinance partner (commission for you).
- **Insurance renewal referral** with a broker partner (commission).
- **Recall check**: warn the client when their model has a manufacturer recall.
- **Social inbox**: Facebook Messenger and Instagram messages in the same inbox as WhatsApp.
- **Family/company garage card**: several cars, one account, one monthly invoice.

## For you (SaaS business)
- ★ **Consumer app "trouver un garage"**: public directory of GaragePro garages and car washes with online booking,
  reviews and prices — brings clients to your customers (and a reason to subscribe).
- ★ **Parts marketplace between garages**: sell surplus or slow stock to other GaragePro garages.
- **Parts ordering commission** with distributors who connect to the "demande de prix" (V8).
- **Partner offers**: lubricant and car-wash product brands sponsor the catalogue or offer discounts to subscribers.
- **Accountant portal**: one accountant follows several garages, downloads exports and e-invoices.
- **White-label for networks** (franchise garages, importers' after-sales networks) with their own brand.
- **Open API + webhooks** for partners and big customers (fleets, insurers).

## Trust label (saved)
- **GaragePro Certifié (trust label)**: earned automatically each month from real data — final invoice close to the
  approved quote, proof photos, part origin shown, written warranty, reviews only from paying clients, complaints
  closed in time, few comebacks. Badge on the garage site, Google and quotes, public "Trust" page, QR to verify.
  Fair public rules with review/appeal, never sold; check consumer-law use of "certifié". Needs many garages.

## Owner assistant (saved)
- **Le Patron (morning briefing + business coach on WhatsApp)**: each morning a short WhatsApp message or voice note
  to the owner: yesterday (cars, invoiced, collected), money to collect with reminder buttons, today (appointments,
  promised cars, cars waiting for parts), alerts (low stock, bad review), one concrete advice with a button (e.g.
  launch a campaign), team efficiency and comebacks. The owner can ask questions in Darija/French ("who hasn't
  paid?", "which car is late?") and confirm actions. Owner/managers only; AI reads and explains, actions need a tap;
  numbers must match the Reports page; push/e-mail fallback; text first, Darija voice later.
  Builds on: reports, "À traiter", unpaid invoices, payment promises, delays, stock alerts, reviews, efficiency (Lot 5),
  AI providers; T6, X1, Saisons. Premium "Patron" add-on or included in higher plans.

## Car sales (saved)
- **Reprise & Revente (help clients sell or change their car)**: "Je veux vendre ma voiture" button; paid sale
  inspection (checklist + OBD scan, red/orange/green with photos); proven history with the owner's consent (repairs,
  km readings, running warranty); one-click listing in the existing showroom with a "History verified by GaragePro"
  badge and buyer messaging; optional trade-in offer; commission (dépôt-vente) or fixed fee; free 1-month check for the
  buyer, who becomes a client. Check first: owner consent (INPDP), inspection = state on that day (not a guarantee),
  Tunisian rules for consignment / buying-reselling (registration transfer, dealer VAT), "first km reading on…".
  Builds on: showroom + messaging (V9), vehicle QR and history, inspections, OBD, warranties, client app; C3, C1,
  vehicle passport and pre-purchase inspection ideas. Partners: banks/leasing, insurers.

## Remote diagnosis (saved)
- **Diagnostic à distance (show the problem before coming)**: "Montrer le problème" button (app, website, WhatsApp)
  by type (noise, warning light, smoke, leak, vibration); guided 30–60 s recording; optional AI first sort (warning
  light from photo, urgency, likely job) always confirmed by a mechanic; mechanic answer queue (urgency, cause, price
  range, parts to order); optional booked 5-minute video call; slot matched to job duration, parts ordered before the
  car arrives; video kept in the work order. Free or small paid "remote opinion".
  Check first: "first opinion, to be confirmed" (never final, esp. brakes/steering); safety message "stop the car" for
  smoke/burning/brakes/red light; private videos with consent and auto-deletion; compress and limit length; answer
  hours and daily limit for mechanics.
  Builds on: photo/video upload, client app and messaging, AI guided diagnosis, booking, missing parts; T6, C6, X1.
